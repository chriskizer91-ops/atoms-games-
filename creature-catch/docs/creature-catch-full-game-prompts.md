# Creature Catch: Full Game Image Pack

These are the images that would round out the full game, from the Forest to the finale. Everything for the first three areas is already in, and every creature now has art. This pack covers what the other six regions, the story, and the post-game still need.

Each group is marked:
- **Must:** the full game needs these.
- **Story:** depends on the story choice (see section 6). These prompts follow "The Fading Moon", which I recommend.
- **Nice:** makes the game richer, but code can cover it.

## How to generate

- **Background:** use the flat magenta #FF00FF background for anything cut out (people, buildings, props). White works too.
- **Style:** keep "Game Boy Advance JRPG style, bold dark outline, limited 16-color palette, flat shading" so the new art matches what you have.
- **File names:** keep the name in each prompt's heading as the file name (for example `granny-moss-hut.png`), so the import tool can place it.
- **Sheets:** ask for "each as a separate sprite, evenly spaced". The tool cuts sheets apart.

## 6. Story and finale: "The Fading Moon" (Story)

**The story in four lines:** The moon over the land has been dimming, and frightened creatures have gone into hiding in the tall grass. Elder Willow sends you out to find out why. Region by region the guides tell you more, until the trail leads to the Moon Altar in the Night Ruins. There, the moon's guardian, dimmed and confused by the fading light, must be calmed in battle (and maybe caught) to bring the moon back.

If you pick another story (option 2 or 3 in the decisions doc), skip this section and I'll write prompts for that one instead.
