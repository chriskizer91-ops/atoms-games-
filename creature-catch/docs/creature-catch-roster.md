## How the numbers are made

- **Base total:** starters 210 / 330 / 450. Base forms of 2-stage lines 170 + 10 per region step (Grassland 190 up to Night Ruins 260). Evolved forms 1.6x their base. The vampire line is 240 / 340 / 450. Solo 195 + 16 per region step (Meadow 211 up to Night Ruins 339; Moonlynx +20).
- **Shape:** the total is split over HP / Attack / Defense / Speed by a profile:

| Profile | HP | Atk | Def | Spd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bal | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| tank | 1.15 | 0.85 | 1.35 | 0.65 |
| wall | 1.4 | 0.7 | 1.2 | 0.7 |
| bulk | 1.3 | 1.05 | 1 | 0.65 |
| atk | 0.95 | 1.3 | 0.85 | 0.9 |
| fast | 0.85 | 1.05 | 0.8 | 1.3 |
| glass | 0.8 | 1.2 | 0.75 | 1.25 |

- **Stats at a level:** `HP = floor(2 x base x Lv / 100) + Lv + 10`, other stats `floor(2 x base x Lv / 100) + 5`.
- **Learnset pattern:** Lv 1 Tackle + type basic (starters: Tackle + Growl, with their type basic at Lv 6, so the first rival fight is even), Lv 4 a stat buff, Lv 8 type utility, Lv 11 (starters) or 16 signature, Lv 12 second-type basic (or Growl), Lv 20 type strong, Lv 26 second-type strong (or Body Slam), Lv 34 another buff. Normal types: Tackle, Growl, buff, signature at 9, buff, Body Slam at 20. An evolved form keeps its line's moves and picks up its new type's moves on evolving. A wild creature knows its two newest attacks plus its newest other moves, up to 4.
- **XP yield:** base total / 4, times the fainted creature's level / 5 (x1.5 against a trainer).
