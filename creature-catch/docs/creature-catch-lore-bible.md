# Creature Catch: The Lore Bible

Everything about the world of Creature Catch in one place: the story from the first dim night to the Balance, every place in the order you reach it, the people you meet there, and **all 175 creatures**, including the 44 added in v11 (his 39 Evolution Lab picks and the family's 5 designs). This is the final creature list for this stage of the game.

This file is made from the game's own data, so the creatures, places and lore here are exactly what's in the game. The v11 creatures are marked **★**. For rules and numbers, `creature-catch-decisions-2.md` wins. For the world and the story, this file wins.

## 1. The world in one page

**Tone.** Gentle and kind. Nobody in this world is evil. The trouble is always a scared or lonely creature that needs a friend, and the answer is always kindness. The humor is soft: grumpy geese, a forgetful chipmunk, a sloth covered in flowers.

**The land** is one long road from Home Village in the south to the Night Ruins in the north, with Copperbell Town halfway along. Side paths lead off the road to orchards, groves, coves, an island, a mine, hot springs, a bayou and an underground library. Creatures hide in the tall grass (or moss, reeds, snow drifts, rune moss), and some only come out by day or at night.

**The Ancients** were the first people who learned that creatures weren't wild beasts, only shy. They left gifts at the edge of the meadows until a creature finally sat beside one of them under the Grandmother Oak: the first friendship. The Ancients built the Moon Altar, the city now called the Night Ruins, the Archive beneath it and the Dawn Temple under the desert, and the Pebbleton golems that still guard their gates. Glyphlings are letters that floated off their walls, and Clocktoise kept their time.

**The capture cube** was the Ancients' way to carry their friends on long journeys. They made the first one on the shore of the great lake: purple for the night sky, blue for the water, and a glowing seam where the two meet, like the horizon at dusk. A creature rests inside it as if it were home.

**The three guardians** keep the world turning: **Solarion** (Fire / Lumen), a golden winged lion, carries the sun by day; **Lunarion** (Lumen), a silver winged wolf with wings full of stars, keeps the moon alight by night; and **Gloaming** (Shadow), small and soft as smoke, is the dusk, the quiet hour that tucks the world in. Solarion and Lunarion never meet, because one always rises as the other sets. Gloaming carried messages between them and was always a little alone, so the Ancients (and later Copperbell, with its Dusk Bell) rang a bell each evening to tell it goodnight.

**Moonborn and Keepers.** Every full moon, the world imagines something new: a child draws a shape by lantern light, and somewhere in the tall grass a creature that never existed before opens its eyes. The game is ready for family-drawn **Moonborn** (who turn up anywhere) and **Keepers** (one-of-a-kind guardians of each side area's landmark). The family's final designs live in ordinary homes instead (the ice cream pup in Frostpeak Pass, and two new evolutions for the Meadow Path), so the Keeper landmarks keep telling their legends with no battle.

## 2. The story

### 2.1 Before the game: the first Long Dusk

1. A thousand years ago, Solarion grew tired and lay down to sleep beneath the desert. Its warm dreams made the oasis bloom. The Ancients built the Dawn Temple around it and promised to wake it when the world needed it.
2. With the sun asleep, Gloaming had only the moon to visit. It held on tighter and tighter until it slipped inside Lunarion's tired heart, and the moon grew dim. The Ancients called those dark years **the Long Dusk**.
3. They ended it with kindness, battling gently, the way you play with a scared friend, until Gloaming let go. Ashamed, it fled deep under the earth. The Ancients sealed a door so it could rest and carved a promise on it: *"One day, someone will come and bring you into the light."*
4. Over the centuries Gloaming grew lonely again. Granny Moss saw it slip into the moon once, briefly, when she was a little girl. Now it has happened again, and this time the moon isn't getting better on its own.

### 2.2 The Fading Moon (the main story)

**The opening.** For many nights the moon over Home Village has been growing dimmer. The villagers worry, and out in the tall grass the creatures hide and tremble. On the day you get your first creature, Elder Willow offers three: Drizzlon (Water), Mossback (Plant) and Embroak (Fire). His granddaughter **Juniper** bursts in, takes the one that beats yours, and challenges you on the spot. Then Willow tells the old legend: Lunarion flew up from the Moon Altar each night and the moon shone bright. Now it fades a little more every night. A trainer with a good heart might reach the altar and find out why.

**The road north.** Each region has a guide who keeps its trail. Win their trial and they open the way, give you a trail charm, and tell you a piece of the moon's story:

| Guide | Place | Opens | What they tell you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Farmer Bramble | Grassland | the fallen log | Granny always said the moon helps the crops grow, and now they grow slow at night. (He also hands you the Vault Key from Hazel.) |
| Ranger Oakley | Forest | the rope bridge | The owls are too scared to fly; the oldest trees remember a silver wolf with wings that watched over the night. |
| Fisher Gus | Lakeshore | the rockslide | The tides have gone wrong, and on full-moon nights a silver wolf used to drink from the lake. |
| Dusty | Crystal Cave | the sand-choked tunnel | The crystals glow with the full moon and are going dark one by one; old carvings show a wolf, a crescent moon and an altar in the northern ruins. He gives you the Wooden Whistle (Fly). |
| Safi | Sunscorch Desert | the sandstorm | One star is missing: the Moon's Eye, which rests in the guardian's crest. If the guardian hurts, so does its star. |
| Frida | Frostpeak Pass | the ice wall | From the summit you can see a shadow sitting over the ruins at night. Something up there is hurting. |
| Granny Moss | Murkmire Swamp | the broken boardwalk | She saw this as a girl: the guardian grew tired and the shadow crept into it. Calm it, don't beat it; battle with a kind heart. |
| Night Watchman Corvin | Night Ruins | the rune gate | Beyond his rune gate is the Moon Altar. He gives you the Master Cube, made by the Ancients "for this night." |

**Juniper** stays a step ahead the whole way, with a team built against your starter: in the Forest, the Crystal Cave, high on the snowy pass, and at the Night Ruins. Along the road the Wandering Merchant Tobias sells Ultra Cubes, and every outpost keeper heals you and opens the Cube Vault.

**The finale.** At the Moon Altar the moon is almost gone. A huge shadow rises: Lunarion, its crescent crest cracked and flickering, eyes wild with fear. You can't run and you can't catch it. Battle it gently until the shadow pours out of it like smoke and drifts away into the night (that's Gloaming leaving), then Lunarion, calm again, can be caught. The moon shines full; back home the villagers light lanterns and every kind of creature comes out of the grass to see. The credits roll, and the **Moonlit Spire** opens beside the altar.

### 2.3 After the story: the Long Dusk returns

1. **The hint.** Elder Willow says pages of his book are still missing, and that the shadow that left Lunarion is still out there, alone. With 8 pages found, he notices a golden sun on page 8 and thinks of Safi's grandmother at the oasis.
2. **The Dawn Temple.** At the oasis, Grandmother Amani opens the hidden stairs under the water (once the moon is restored and 8 pages are found). Three mirrors send sunlight down the halls to the Sun Altar, where page 11 lies.
3. **Solarion wakes,** groggy and grumpy, not angry, only confused after a thousand years. Calm it in battle, then it can be caught (or it waits on the altar). It gives you the **Sun Ember**: "Find the little one who hides below. Tell it it was never forgotten."
4. **The sealed door.** In Glimmer Mine, the Sun Ember melts the seal on the Ancient Door while Grit and Dusty watch.
5. **The Hollow Deep.** Down in the dark, Gloaming is curled up small at the Dusk Pool, sure it ruined everything. The battle is gentle, to a lullaby ("Gloaming flinches away," "Gloaming looks at you for the first time"). Calm, it gives you page 12, which it had been holding all along. Then it can be caught, or it waits at the pool.
6. **The Dusk Bell rings again** in Copperbell at dusk, its crack healed.
7. **The Balance.** At the top of the Moonlit Spire at dusk, all three guardians meet for the first time in a thousand years, whether or not you caught them. Elder Willow completes the book and names you **Keeper of the Long Dusk**. Cubesmith Ada can make **Dusk Cubes** now, and Moonborn creatures turn up twice as often. A second credits roll thanks everyone who drew a creature.

## 3. The Lost Pages: *The Long Dusk*

Elder Willow's book has 12 pages scattered across the land. He gives 5 Great Cubes for 4 pages, 3 Ultra Cubes for 8, and 5 more Ultra Cubes for all 12 after the Balance.

| Page | Title | Where | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strangers | Home Village | from Tinker, who found it at the bottom of the well (show him 3 different creatures) |
| 2 | Gifts | Willowbrook Orchard | in the hollow of an apple tree |
| 3 | The First Friend | Whispering Grove | from the Grandmother Oak |
| 4 | The First Cube | Driftwood Cove | in a message in a bottle on the beach |
| 5 | Two Guardians | Lantern Isle | from Keeper Wren at the top of the lighthouse |
| 6 | The Little One | Copperbell Town | from Professor Hollis, once you've read all three museum plaques |
| 7 | Messages | Glimmer Mine | in a miner's old lunchbox in a side tunnel |
| 8 | The Sleeping Sun | Steamvent Springs | from Auntie Yuzu, who's been using it as a coaster |
| 9 | The Long Dusk | Firefly Bayou | on a hidden stump the fireflies light up, at night only |
| 10 | Kindness | Ancients' Archive | from Archivist Ilsa, after three Glyphlings make the blank wall speak |
| 11 | To Whoever Wakes Me | Dawn Temple | on the Sun Altar |
| 12 | Beginnings | Hollow Deep | from Gloaming, once it's calm |

**1. Strangers.** In the oldest days, people and creatures were strangers. The creatures hid in the tall grass, and the people were afraid of the dark between the trees. Neither knew the other was only shy.

**2. Gifts.** The first people to cross the line between field and grass were called the Ancients. They left small gifts at the edge of the meadows: apples, seeds, smooth shining stones. In the morning the gifts were gone, and a little path had been trampled through the grass.

**3. The First Friend.** Under the oldest oak, a girl of the Ancients sat very still for a whole day. At sunset a small creature crept out and sat beside her. Neither of them moved until the stars came out. That was the first friendship, and the oak remembers it still.

**4. The First Cube.** The Ancients wished to carry their friends on long journeys. On the shore of the great lake they shaped the first cube: purple for the night sky, blue for the water, and a seam of light where the two meet, like the horizon at dusk. A creature rests inside it as if it were home.

**5. Two Guardians.** Two guardians watched over the world. Solarion, the golden lion, carried the sun across the sky. Lunarion, the silver wolf, kept the moon alight through the night. They never met, for one always rose as the other set.

**6. The Little One.** Between the day and the night there is one quiet hour, and it belongs to a third guardian, soft as smoke. The Ancients named it Gloaming. It tucks the world in for the night. It has no light of its own, so it borrows a little from each of the others.

**7. Messages.** Gloaming carried messages between the sun and the moon: a good morning from one, a good night from the other. It loved them both. But it was always the one in between, and it was always a little alone. The Ancients rang a bell each evening so it would know someone was thinking of it.

**8. The Sleeping Sun.** Once the sun guardian grew tired and lay down to sleep beneath the desert sands. Its warm dreams made a spring bloom in the dunes. The Ancients built a temple around it and made a promise: when the world needs you, we will wake you.

**9. The Long Dusk.** With the sun asleep, Gloaming had only the moon to visit. It held on tighter and tighter, until it slipped inside Lunarion's tired heart. The moon grew dim, night after night. The Ancients called those dark years the Long Dusk.

**10. Kindness.** The Ancients did not end the Long Dusk with cubes or with anger. They battled gently, the way you play with a scared friend, until Gloaming let go. Afterward it fled deep under the earth, ashamed. The Ancients sealed a door to let it rest and carved a promise on the stone: one day, someone will come and bring you into the light.

**11. To Whoever Wakes Me.** I am Solarion. The world turns because three of us keep it: the moon for dreaming, the sun for growing, and the dusk for resting. Find the little one who hides below. Tell it that it was never forgotten.

**12. Beginnings.** Every full moon, the world imagines something new. A child draws a shape by lantern light, and somewhere in the tall grass a creature that never existed before opens its eyes. That is why the cube's seam glows. The world is always beginning.

## 4. Places, people and creatures

In the order you reach them. Each place lists the creature families you can **first** meet there (a whole family is listed where you first meet any of it, the same way the Creature Log numbers them), then the ones from earlier places that also live there. **★** marks the creatures added in v11.

### 4.1 Home Village

The south end of the land, where every journey starts.

A small village of mossy roofs. Elder Willow's big house, Pip's Cube Shop, Hazel's Healing Hut, Rosa's Inn and your own home. Tinker found the first Lost Page at the bottom of the village well.

**People:** Elder Willow (gives you your first creature), Juniper (your rival, Willow's granddaughter), Hazel (Healing Hut), Pip (Cube Shop), Rosa (the Inn), Tinker (a village kid).  
**Lost Page 1** ("Strangers"): from Tinker, who found it at the bottom of the well (show him 3 different creatures).

**First found here:**

- **Drizzlon → Rapidrake → Tsunadrake** (A starter)
  - **Drizzlon** · Water: A baby water dragon that hatches from eggs left in spring rain puddles. The droplet on its tail never dries out, and it flicks it at friends to say hello.
  - **Rapidrake** · Water (from Drizzlon at Lv 14): Its fin-wings grow long enough to skim across lakes like a skipped stone. It practices racing the river current every morning and usually wins.
  - **Tsunadrake** · Water / Bolt (from Rapidrake at Lv 30): When it beats its wave-shaped wings, storms gather out of a clear sky. The yellow marks on its body crackle with the lightning it swallows from those storms.
- **Mossback → Thornshell → Elderoak** (A starter)
  - **Mossback** · Plant: A sleepy turtle whose shell grows a little bush, and it is very proud of its single pink flower. It naps in sunny spots so the bush can grow.
  - **Thornshell** · Plant (from Mossback at Lv 14): Its bush has thickened into a thorny thicket that keeps hungry creatures away. Birds still nest in it, and Thornshell lets them.
  - **Elderoak** · Plant / Earth (from Thornshell at Lv 30): An oak tree has rooted in its rocky shell. Old stories say whole villages once grew up in the shade of a sleeping Elderoak without ever knowing it was alive.
- **Embroak → Cinderfrog → Infernotoad** (A starter)
  - **Embroak** · Fire: A cheeky fire frog that keeps a tiny ember on the tip of its tongue. It loves to sneak up and pop that ember near people's ears.
  - **Cinderfrog** · Fire (from Embroak at Lv 14): Flames now run all down its back, and it hops between hot rocks to stay toasty. Its croak sounds like a crackling campfire.
  - **Infernotoad** · Fire / Earth (from Cinderfrog at Lv 30): Its skin has cooled into charcoal rock, cracked with glowing lava veins. When it slams down, the ground around it bakes hard as brick.

### 4.2 Meadow Path

The first path north out of Home Village. Wild creatures Lv 2 to 4. On the main road.

Flowers and short grass. Only Normal creatures live here, so no starter has it too easy or too hard on its first walk.

**First found here:**

- **Clovermouse → ★ Clove Tastical** (Common)
  - **Clovermouse** · Normal: It wears a four-leaf clover like a hat and is said to bring good luck to anyone who sees it first. It is shy and scurries off at the first footstep.
  - ★ **Clove Tastical** *(his design)* · Plant / Normal (from Clovermouse at Lv 18): It wraps itself in a cloak of four-leaf clovers. Meeting one is said to bring a whole day of good luck.
- **Hopsprout → ★ Hoppin Pop** (Common)
  - **Hopsprout** · Normal: The two-leaf sprout on its head turns to follow the sun all day. It can leap ten times its own height to snatch a falling seed.
  - ★ **Hoppin Pop** *(his design)* · Plant (from Hopsprout at Lv 20): Its sprout has bloomed into a crown of flowers, and its legs are stronger than ever. It tries to hop higher each time it hops, and one day it's sure it will reach the moon.
- **Puffmunk → Stashmunk** (Uncommon, day only)
  - **Puffmunk** · Normal: A chipmunk that stuffs its cheeks so full of berries it can barely see over them. It hides spare berries all over the meadow and forgets where.
  - **Stashmunk** · Normal (from Puffmunk at Lv 16): It digs secret pantries under the meadow and guards them fiercely. Lost travelers have made it through whole winters on its forgotten stashes.
- **Fleecling → Cloudram → ★ Tempestram** (Rare, day only)
  - **Fleecling** · Normal: A lamb whose wool is a real little cloud. On hot days it rains a few drops on itself to cool off.
  - **Cloudram** · Normal / Bolt (from Fleecling at Lv 22): Its cloud wool has become a thunderhead. When it charges, thunder rolls across the meadow before it arrives.
  - ★ **Tempestram** *(new in v11)* · Normal / Bolt (from Cloudram at Lv 36): Its wool is a whole storm. Farmers watch for it in dry summers, because wherever Tempestram grazes, rain follows, and a rainbow comes after.

### 4.3 Grassland

North of the Meadow Path. Wild creatures Lv 4 to 7. On the main road.

Wide open fields, the first outpost (Fern keeps it), and Farmer Bramble's farm. A fallen log blocks the trail north until you pass his trial.

**People:** Farmer Bramble (the first guide), Fern (outpost).  
**Guide's trial:** Farmer Bramble opens the fallen log.

**First found here:**

- **Tuftail → Verdantail → ★ Sylvantail** (Common)
  - **Tuftail** · Plant: This fox's tail is a bundle of living grass. It hides in tall grass with only the tip showing, and you only spot it when the tail blinks.
  - **Verdantail** · Plant (from Tuftail at Lv 18): Flowers bloom in its huge grassy tail, and they change with the seasons. A field it naps in grows greener overnight.
  - ★ **Sylvantail** *(new in v11)* · Plant / Lumen (from Verdantail at Lv 34): A forest spirit with three leafy tails. Flowers sprout in its footprints, and in spring you can follow its trail across the whole Grassland.
- **Pebblepup → Boulderhound → ★ Cragmastiff** (Common)
  - **Pebblepup** · Earth: A playful puppy with rocky patches in its fur and a pebble stuck to its nose that it refuses to let go of. It fetches anything, especially rocks.
  - **Boulderhound** · Earth (from Pebblepup at Lv 18): Boulder armor covers its shoulders and back, and it stands guard over farms through the night. One bark can be heard across the whole valley.
  - ★ **Cragmastiff** *(new in v11)* · Earth / Normal (from Boulderhound at Lv 34): It's so big that birds mistake it for a mountain. A little waterfall runs down its back, and it wags its tail so hard the ground shakes.
- **Bumblop → Bumblazer → ★ Queenbuzz** (Uncommon)
  - **Bumblop** · Bolt: Half bee, half bunny, all fluff. It buzzes clumsily from flower to flower and gives off tiny sparks of static when it is excited.
  - **Bumblazer** · Bolt (from Bumblop at Lv 20): Its wings crackle with electricity and it zips about too fast to follow. A swarm of Bumblazer over a field means a thunderstorm is coming.
  - ★ **Queenbuzz** *(new in v11)* · Bolt / Plant (from Bumblazer at Lv 36): The queen of every Bumblop hive. When it hums, every flower in the meadow turns to face it and the whole hive crackles with static.
- **Dewt → Monsoonewt → ★ Typhoonewt** (Uncommon, day only)
  - **Dewt** · Water: A newt made of morning dew. It sparkles on the grass at sunrise and turns to mist if you startle it.
  - **Monsoonewt** · Water (from Dewt at Lv 20): It carries a little raincloud over its head everywhere it goes. Farmers welcome it in dry summers.
  - ★ **Typhoonewt** *(new in v11)* · Water / Bolt (from Monsoonewt at Lv 36): It rides a storm cloud like a little boat. After it passes, the air smells like rain and there is always a rainbow.
- **Dandylion → Sunmane** (Rare)
  - **Dandylion** · Fire: A lion cub with a mane of dandelion puffs. When it roars, the puffs scatter on the wind, and new dandelions sprout wherever they land.
  - **Sunmane** · Fire / Lumen (from Dandylion with a Sun Shard): Touched by a Sun Shard, its mane blazes like the summer sun. It is said to appear on the longest day of the year to warm the fields.

### 4.4 Willowbrook Orchard

East of the Grassland. Wild creatures Lv 5 to 8. Side area.

Sunny rows of apple trees, a red barn with a cider press, beehives and a scarecrow. Marigold (Farmer Bramble's sister) runs it with her son Russet.

**People:** Marigold, Russet.  
**Landmark:** the Oldest Apple Tree.  
**Lost Page 2** ("Gifts"): in the hollow of an apple tree.

**First found here:**

- **Wormapple** (Common, day only)
  - **Wormapple** · Plant / Normal: A worm that lives inside a red apple and wears it like a house. It pops out to say hello and pops back in when it's shy. Marigold thought it was eating her apples, but it's actually chasing the pests away.
- **Scarecrowl → ★ Scarecrown** (Uncommon, night only)
  - **Scarecrowl** · Shadow: A crow wearing a tattered scarecrow hat. Marigold put up the scarecrow to keep it away, and it moved right in.
  - ★ **Scarecrown** *(new in v11)* · Shadow / Plant (from Scarecrowl at Lv 22): The crow became the scarecrow. Now it stands in Willowbrook Orchard at night, and all the crows come to sit on its arms and tell it the news.

**Also here:** Puffmunk, Bumblop, Dandylion.

### 4.5 Forest

North of the Grassland, past Farmer Bramble's log. Wild creatures Lv 5 to 10. On the main road.

Tall trees and falling leaves around the Elder Tree. Owls should fill it at night, but they've been too scared to fly since the moon began to dim.

**People:** Ranger Oakley (guide), Basil (outpost).  
**Landmark:** the Elder Tree.  
**Guide's trial:** Ranger Oakley opens the rope bridge.

**First found here:**

- **Barkbeetle → ★ Timberbeetle** (Common)
  - **Barkbeetle** · Earth: Its shell is so much like tree bark that woodcutters have tried to chop it. It munches fallen logs and leaves the forest floor tidy.
  - ★ **Timberbeetle** *(new in v11)* · Earth / Plant (from Barkbeetle at Lv 22): Its shell has become a mossy tree stump with a little tree growing on top. Squirrels have lived on its back for generations.
- **Mushling → ★ Mushroyal** (Common)
  - **Mushling** · Shadow: A mushroom creature that peeks out from under its spotted cap and darts back if you look at it. Its spores can make you sleepy, so don't sniff too close.
  - ★ **Mushroyal** *(new in v11)* · Shadow / Plant (from Mushling at Lv 20): Its cap is so wide that little Mushlings nap under it when it rains. It's the oldest mushroom in the Forest and only moves when it absolutely must.
- **Owlcorn → Timberowl → ★ Oakenowl** (Uncommon)
  - **Owlcorn** · Plant: It wears a giant acorn cap as a hat and never takes it off, even to sleep. It hoards acorns in hollow trees and forgets most of them, which is how new oaks grow.
  - **Timberowl** · Plant (from Owlcorn at Lv 22): Its feathers have hardened like bark and its acorn crown sprouts fresh oak leaves. Strong enough to carry a person, it is the bird that answers the Wooden Whistle.
  - ★ **Oakenowl** *(new in v11)* · Plant / Normal (from Timberowl at Lv 40): When it sits still it looks exactly like an old oak tree. Travelers have picnicked under one and never known, until it blinked.
- **Mothlight → Radimoth** (Uncommon)
  - **Mothlight** · Lumen: A pale moth whose wings glow softly in the dark. Lost travelers follow its light, and it always leads them to a path.
  - **Radimoth** · Lumen (from Mothlight with a Moon Shard): Bathed in moonlight from a Moon Shard, its wings now shine with sunburst patterns. Its glow keeps shadows away from anyone it decides to protect.
- **Burrhog → ★ Burrboar** (Uncommon)
  - **Burrhog** · Plant / Earth: A hedgehog whose spines are sticky burrs. Seeds catch in them as it rolls, so it plants half the forest without meaning to.
  - ★ **Burrboar** *(new in v11)* · Plant / Earth (from Burrhog at Lv 22): It rolls through the Forest like a spiky wheel, and every seed stuck to it gets planted somewhere new. Whole groves grew where Burrboar likes to roll.
- **Budstag → ★ Bloomstag** (Rare)
  - **Budstag** · Plant: A young deer whose branch-like antlers bud with pink blossoms each spring. Where it walks, flowers open a little earlier.
  - ★ **Bloomstag** *(new in v11)* · Plant / Lumen (from Budstag at Lv 24): Its antlers have grown into a blooming tree. Birds nest in them every spring, and Bloomstag walks very carefully so it never wakes the chicks.

### 4.7 Lakeshore

North of the Forest, over Ranger Oakley's rope bridge. Wild creatures Lv 8 to 13. On the main road.

A wide lake with docks, reeds and a lighthouse on the point. The Ancients made the very first capture cube on this shore.

**People:** Fisher Gus (guide), Marina (outpost).  
**Landmark:** the lighthouse.  
**Guide's trial:** Fisher Gus opens the rockslide.

**First found here:**

- **Bubblefin → Surgefin → ★ Leviafin** (Common)
  - **Bubblefin** · Water: A tiny round fish that blows bubbles when it's happy, which is nearly always. Children chase its bubbles along the shore.
  - **Surgefin** · Water (from Bubblefin at Lv 16): It has grown sleek and fast, with a spiky fin and a toothy grin. It rides the strongest currents just for fun.
  - ★ **Leviafin** *(new in v11)* · Water (from Surgefin at Lv 34): The gentle giant of the lake. It swims slowly with its mouth open, and little Bubblefin ride along beside it like a parade.
- **Crabbit → Rampacrab → ★ Paladcrab** (Common)
  - **Crabbit** · Water: A crab with long rabbit ears that it uses to listen for danger under the sand. It hops sideways faster than you'd expect.
  - **Rampacrab** · Water / Earth (from Crabbit at Lv 20): Its ears and shell are armored with stone, and it builds little sea walls out of rocks. Fisher Gus calls it the harbor's doorman.

## 6. How they work in the game

- **Nobody's creature got weaker.** A creature that gained a form kept the stats it had. A new second form gets the usual evolved budget (1.6 times a first form's), and a new third form a third more than the form before it, at most 450 like the other three-form families. Creatures already caught in a save picked this up on their own. The data is in `src/js/12c-data-final.js`.
- **Moves.** Each new form shares its family's signature move and learns the rest from the usual pattern. The family's designs brought three new moves: **Lucky Leaf** (Clove Tastical, Plant, often lands a critical hit), **Fantastic Hop** (Hoppin Pop, Plant, raises its Speed every time, so it hops higher each time it hops) and **Sprink Magick** (the ice cream pup's family, Frost).
- **Numbers** follow the journey (`src/js/13c-field-order.js`): each new form sits right after its family, Crescub just before Moonlynx, and the ice cream pup with Frostpeak Pass. The Creature Log and the Field Guide share them.
- **Where they turn up.** ? Pop lives in Frostpeak Pass and evolves at Lv 22, so you meet ? Pop at Lv 20 and 21 and Pup Pop from 22. Crescub took Moonlynx's place in the Night Ruins, and Moonlynx shows up there by level, from Lv 30. Everything else comes by evolving, and since a wild creature past its evolution level is met evolved, some new forms live in the wild too: Cragmastiff in Moonlit Spire, Oakenowl in Moonlit Spire, Leviafin in Moonlit Spire, Furnadillo in Dawn Temple, Stalagwing in Hollow Deep.
- **Runescroll and the Archive wall.** Ilsa's wall needs three Glyphlings, and a Runescroll is three Glyphlings joined together, so it counts as three.
- **Credits.** The second credits roll lists A.S.K. under "Creatures drawn by."
