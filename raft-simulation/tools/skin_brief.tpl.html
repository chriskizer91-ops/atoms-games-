Castaway refit

The Farm lad and the Pie seller, re-cut as castaways. Their new shapes are built. What they need now is paint: one picture per outfit, made in ChatGPT from the prompts below, which I then wrap onto the 3D models.

How it works

  1. Open a cardEach card below is one picture to make.
  2. Attach its picturesIn the order shown. They are all in the zip I sent; or press and hold (right-click on a laptop) a picture here to save it.
  3. Copy the promptPaste it into ChatGPT with the pictures attached.
  4. Save and send backSave the result under the name on the card and send the files to me.

Why a grey guide? The finished painting is laid back onto the model by position: every point of the body takes the colour at its own place on the sheet, front from the left figure, back from the right. So the one thing that matters is that the painted figures stay exactly where the grey ones are. If a result has moved, grown or changed the pose, ask ChatGPT to redo it “keeping both figures exactly on the guide”.

What is fixed and what is free. The shapes are fixed (hat, vest, sash, cuffs: whatever is in the guide). Everything painted on them is free: colours, patterns, patches, stains, stitching. Change any line in a prompt to taste.
