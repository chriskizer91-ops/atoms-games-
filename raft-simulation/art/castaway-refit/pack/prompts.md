## The castaway man (was the Farm lad)

### castaway-man-A: Washed ashore

Attach: castaway-man-guide.png, castaway-man-A-colours.png, style-farm-lad.jpg, palette.png

```
Attach four pictures, in this order:
1. castaway-man-guide.png
2. castaway-man-A-colours.png
3. style-farm-lad.jpg
4. palette.png

Paint over picture 1 and give me one finished picture, 1536 x 1024 pixels (landscape, 3 wide by 2 tall).

Picture 1 is a grey guide of one person shown twice: from the front on the left half, from behind on the right half, standing with the arms a little out from the sides. It is a costume sheet for a 3D game character: the finished painting is wrapped back onto the 3D model by position, so every shape must stay exactly where it is.

Picture 2 is the same guide with every part filled in the base colour it should end up. Use it to see which grey shape is which part and what colour it is. Do not copy its flat look: paint real, worn cloth, leather, rope and skin over it.

Picture 3 is a finished sheet of this same person in the clothes they wore before. Match its hand-painted style, its level of detail, its flat even light and its plain grey background. Take the style from it, not the clothes.

Picture 4 is the game's colour card. Where I give no colour, choose from it, so the character belongs in the game's world: a small raft on a warm, bright tropical sea.

WHO: A young farmhand of about seventeen, a few weeks adrift on a raft in a warm sea. Everything he wears is what he had on, mended with what the sea gave him. Cheerful, sunburnt, practical.

THE SHAPES IN THE GUIDE (keep every one, add none): a wide straw hat with a band; short hair showing under it at the sides and back; a kerchief knotted at the throat with one short tail on the chest; a loose shirt with the sleeves rolled into thick cuffs above the elbow and its tail hanging out over the hips; bare forearms and hands; a short open vest that ends at the waist; a rope belt of two turns with a knot and a short hanging end at his left hip (the right side of the front view); a sheath knife on his right hip (the left side of the front view); narrow trousers with a thick turn-up at mid-shin; below it the lower shin and foot are bound in cloth wraps (drawn as a boot shape in the guide) with a chunky sole.

WHAT TO PAINT, part by part:
- HAT: golden straw (#d9b867) woven in rings, the rings a shade darker (#b08f45), the straw split and frayed along the edge of the brim, sun-bleached paler on top. Round the crown a band of faded indigo cloth (#4a5f7a) with a fish-hook stuck through it.
- HAIR: short, sandy (#c9a66b), stiff with salt, showing below the hat at the sides and on the nape.
- KERCHIEF: faded brick red (#b5523a) cotton, salt-stained paler along its folds, knotted at the throat, one short tail on the chest.
- SHIRT: unbleached linen (#d9ccab) gone soft and grey-cream, with wavy white salt tide-lines across the chest and back, a small three-cornered tear at one shoulder, the collar open. The sleeves are rolled into fat cuffs above the elbow. The shirt tail hangs out below the vest and belt, its hem ragged, one corner darker where it is still damp.
- VEST: cut from old sailcloth, warm tan canvas (#9b8257), unhemmed raw edges, sewn with big uneven stitches of dark tarred twine. On its back, a square patch of blue sail (#3f74a8) the size of a hand. Two bone toggles on cord loops at the front edge.
- BELT: hemp rope (#b89a62) wound twice round the waist over the shirt, twisted strands clearly drawn, tied in a square knot at his left hip with a frayed end hanging a hand's length.
- KNIFE: on his right hip a dark brown leather sheath (#5a3a24), scuffed pale at the tip, the knife's handle wrapped in tarred cord showing at the top.
- TROUSERS: slate blue-grey canvas (#5d6f73), sun-faded almost to pale grey on the fronts of the thighs, salt-crusted at the knees, a tan patch (#9b8257) stitched on the left thigh. They end at mid-shin in a thick ragged turn-up (#4c5c60, the darker unfaded inside of the cloth).
- FEET: bound in strips of pale sailcloth (#b9a888) wound round and round like bandages from the toes to halfway up the shin, tied with thin cord in a criss-cross, grubby at the toes, with a dark rope sole (#4a3a2c).
- SKIN: forearms and hands lightly tanned with freckles, the knuckles sunburnt pink, a pale band at each wrist where the sleeves used to reach.

WEAR AND WEATHER, over everything: sun-fading on the upper surfaces (hat top, shoulders, the fronts of the thighs), white wavy salt lines where sea water has dried, darker damp at the lowest hems, frayed raw edges, visible weave, stitching, darns and patches. Clean enough to read, never filthy. Every detail at least as wide as a finger so it still shows when the picture is small.

RULES
- Keep both figures exactly where they are and exactly the size they are in picture 1. Trace the guide's outlines: the same hat or headscarf, hair, sleeves, hands, belt, clothes, legs and feet, and the same gaps between the arms and the body. Do not move, resize, crop, turn or re-pose the figures.
- Add nothing outside the grey shapes: nothing held in the hands, nothing hanging, blowing or sticking out past the outline. No new hat brims, capes, bags or weapons.
- The two figures are the same person in the same clothes. Every colour, pattern, patch and stain on the front carries round to the back in the matching place. The right figure is seen from behind: the back of the head, no face. Remember that the person's left is on the right of the front view and on the left of the back view.
- Light both figures flat and evenly from straight ahead, like a costume sheet. No cast shadows, no ground, no glow, no rim light, no dark side, no highlights from a lamp or the sun. The game adds its own light. Fading and stains are part of the cloth; shadows are not.
- Skin: fair, sun-pinked (#d99789) on the forearms, hands and neck.
- Paint the face on the left figure simply. A separate close-up replaces it.
- Fill the whole background with one flat mid-grey, the same as picture 1. No text, no labels, no numbers, no border, no colour swatches.

Save the result as castaway-man-A-body.png
```
