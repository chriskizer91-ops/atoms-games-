# Pass 1: the opening (title, graduation cutscene, degree picker)

Scratch pass name: `opening`. Everything here is presentation. `startCutscene()` -> `cutUpdate(dt)` -> `finishCut()` -> `showDegreeScreen()` is unchanged, `CUT.t`, `CUT_END` and `CUT_CAPS` are untouched, `#cutSkip` skips from any moment, and the degree picker still sets `pickedDegree`, types into `#dField` and enables `#btnAccept` with the same label.

## What changed

### Cutscene (`34-cutscene.js` + new `34b`..`34j` files)
- **A real camera.** The 1280x720 stage is now a world. `CIN_KEYS` is a Hermite spline of (x, y, zoom, roll): a crane-down establishing shot (sky, sun and clock tower first, then the stage and crowd), a push-in and track with the Graduate on the walk, a two-shot, a hold on the hands (rack focus: a pre-blurred twin of the set fades in), a pull back, a tilt up for the diploma, a whip down to the standing crowd (10.5-11.2 s) and back up, and a crane back for the hat toss with a small roll. Layers sit at a depth (0.08 sky ... 1 action ... 1.6 foreground) so zoom and pans parallax automatically. Narrow screens zoom out a little and crop/pan to the action (focus-follow); captions sit in the lower letterbox bar and never cover faces.
- **Set** (all baked once, `34c`/`34d`/`34e`): layered sky with drifting cumulus, sun glare, birds, three tree-textured hill ridges, a campus with a brick clock tower (copper roof, clock face), oaks, iron lamp-posts; the stage has navy drape back wall, a gold-framed banner with the same lettering (FRESH GRAD STATE / CLASS OF 2026, serif + display type, embossed, with a light sweep), velvet curtains made of fold strips with tie-backs, rope and tassels and bullion fringe, a valance with gold braid and tassels, a brass rail, a plank floor with seams, grain, scuffs and a sheen, a lectern (carved panels, laurel medallion, gooseneck mic with a twinkle), urns of flowers, swaying bunting. The audience is three seated tiers (folding chairs, families, sun hats, toddlers on shoulders, blank hand-made signs, people filming on glowing phones), blurred by depth, plus balloons and a blurred foreground row with bokeh. The crowd ripples, then erupts with raised arms and camera flashes at 9.8 s.
- **Cast** (`34f` figure rig, `34f2` choreography): proper proportions (head about 1:4.4), IK arms with bell sleeves, hands with fingers (relax, open, fist, shake, clap, point), gown with pleats/hem/rim light/ambient occlusion baked per costume, gold stole with fringe, two-tone shoes, a contact shadow plus a long cast shadow away from the sun, faces with brows, eyes (blink, laugh), blush, mouth shapes, laugh lines, hair styles (short, long, afro, curly, pony, silver) with a messy variant after the toss, mortarboards with thickness and a damped-pendulum tassel. The Graduate keeps the coral tee collar under the gown. Eight individual classmates (heights, hair, skin tones from the original arrays, one with glasses, one crying happy, one blowing a kiss), they turn their heads to follow the walk, clap, cheer, and throw their caps. The Dean has silver hair, black gown, gold stole, laughs and applauds. The diploma is a rolled sheet with fibres, spiral ends, ribbon bow and wax seal; it passes between the hands (6.9-7.5 s), goes to the chest, then overhead (9.6 s).
- **FX** (`34g`): follow spots with dust motes and floor pools, long sun shafts, bloom on lamps/footlights, camera flashes, a lens flare that only appears when the sun is in frame, mortarboards that tumble in 3D (diamond aspect flips) with trails, a slow-motion window at the toss (`cinWarp`), confetti with flutter in three depth bins (the near bin is large and soft), streamers, sparkles. Reduced motion: no camera shake or hand-held sway, calmer roll, static grain, no strobing flashes (the white flash is capped at 55%).
- **Post** is DOM, not canvas: `#cutGrade` (multiply, cool to warm), `#cutVig` (vignette with a hair of chromatic fringe), `#cutGrain` (24 fps stepped noise tile), `.cut-bars` (animated letterbox), `#cutFlash` (fade-in and white-out). The scene code only sets four CSS variables per frame.
- **Typography and cards**: glass caption plate (speaker in gold mono caps, tinted per speaker, DM Sans text, typewriter caret); the two black cards ("Diploma in hand." / "Now, what does it say?") are Fraunces on a gold hairline over a dark canvas scene (breathing vignette, rising golden motes) with a rolled diploma floating between them. Same ids (`#cutCap`, `#cutCard`) and the same typewriter timings.
- **Sound** (`34h`, built on `SFX.ctx`, quiet, respects `G.sound`): crowd murmur, birds, footsteps locked to the stride, a string-like swell on the walk resolving into a warm chord on the handshake, paper foley, camera shutters, a whoosh at the toss, a big resonant chord under the white flash. `SFX.applause` calls stay as they were.
- **Determinism**: `drawCutScene(ct)` is a pure function of `ct` (seeded RNG only; the head-sprite cache only memoises identical output). Scrubbing `CUT.t` (the tour does) works.

### Optional illustrated art (all behind `ART` checks; missing files are silent no-ops)
- `CUT_SHOTS` (in `34-cutscene.js`): each still cross-fades over the procedural scene in its window with a Ken-Burns move, inside the letterbox (so captions stay clear). The four memory inserts get a warm faded grade. `still-09-sunset` sits behind the "Diploma in hand." card.
- `34i-cinart.js`: `plate-stage-empty` (+ `plate-crowd-back`, `plate-crowd-front`, `plate-podium`) replace the procedural backdrop and set (the real banner lettering is drawn on the blank banner; tune `CIN_PLATE.banner` if the painted banner sits elsewhere). `chars/grad/pose-*` and `chars/dean/pose-*` replace the procedural figures (swap + squash/stretch + bob; the procedural diploma only fills the beats where the pose has none). `chars/ch-05-classmates-{stand,cheer,toss}` replace the line of classmates. Each piece degrades on its own. Placeholder PNGs were generated into the scratch dir to prove every hook and are **not** committed. The paper-doll renderer (`parts-sheet.png`) is not implemented (the poses cover the same need, and on `file://` a sheet cannot be read back to find its parts, so slicing would have to guess the layout).
- Title: `title/hero-wide` / `hero-tall` fade in over the live town in `titleFrame` and then replace it. Degree picker: `ui/diploma` becomes the diploma's paper (the typed field text stays HTML), `ui/paper-cream` textures the offer letter.

### Title (`01-title.css`, body.html `#title`, `11-title-scene.js`)
Giant "Fresh Grad" with a layered extrusion, a chrome split in the fill and a slow light sweep, "Grad" in marigold foil with a gleam; hairline eyebrow; a mortarboard mark whose tassel swings; marigold primary and glass ghost buttons (scoped to `#title`); a refined clock pill; around the live canvas: warm grade, sun glint, drifting golden motes, vignette, grain and a gentle pointer parallax (DOM only, off for touch and reduced motion). 390x844 verified.

### Degree picker (`02-cutscene-degree.css`, `02b-diploma.css`, `34j-cindegree.js`, body.html `#degree`)
Six navy-glass cards with an emblem tile each (code brackets, gear, cross, chart, apple, pan and flame), tinted by the degree's own wall/roof colours, with hover tilt and a glowing selected state; a deckled parchment diploma with paper fibre, an engraved navy double border with corner flourishes, a faint crest, an embossed Fraunces institution line, a calligraphic italic typed field with an ink shimmer and a caret while typing, a gold wax seal with ribbon that is re-stamped on each pick; the offer is a cream job-offer letter (letterhead in the job's colours, emblem, mono pay line); a polished fixed CTA bar. On phones the card list comes first and the diploma scrolls into view after a pick.

## Intentionally untouched
`SFX`/`MUSIC`, the main loop, `G.mode` flow, `boot()`, `finishCut`'s hand-off, every town drawing function (`paintTown` is only called by the title wrapper as before), `.btn`/`.chip-btn` base styles.

## Notes for the integrator
- `body.html`: only the three owned sections changed (`#title` got `.t-fx`, an SVG tassel mark and spans in the `h1`; `#cut` got `.cut-fx`, `.cut-bars`, `#cutFlash`; `#degree` got the diploma ornaments).
- Class names are generic in places (`.emb`, `.typing`, `.pop`, `.art`); a clash with a global `.stamp` rule from the passport CSS bit me once (renamed to `.stamped`). Check new global class names from other passes against these.
- Fonts: Fraunces (italic + 700) is requested for the canvas banner at load (`document.fonts.load`) and the banner is re-baked when it arrives.

## Wishes for other passes
- A shared `FX.sh`-like light vector would let the cutscene reuse it; the cast uses its own `CIN_LIGHT` (upper left).
- If the UI pass restyles `.btn`, the `#title .btn` and `#degree .btn` overrides can be deleted.

## Known limitations
- The cutscene is vector-heavy; in a software-rendered headless browser it costs about 1.1x to 1.9x of the old scene's frame time (see the summary); on a GPU canvas it is light. The scene renders at the display ratio capped to ~1600 px on the long side and quietly drops resolution if frames stay slow for several seconds.
- Stills are drawn with `ART.cover` straight from the PNG; very large stills cost a few ms per frame in software canvases.
- The handshake choreography assumes the Dean on the right and the Graduate on the left. When pose art exists the procedural clasp and scroll are skipped (the poses carry them) except for the beats where a pose has no diploma.
- Pose / plate / classmate-lineup placement uses best-effort constants (`CIN_POSE_H`, `CIN_PLATE`, the lineup rectangle in `cinArtActors`); they were verified with placeholder images that follow the framing rules in IMAGE_PROMPTS.md, so expect to nudge them once the real art lands.
