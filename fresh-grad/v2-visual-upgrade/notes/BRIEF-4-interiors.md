## Where the game currently is (baseline: `*-int-*` and `*-school-*` shots, day and night)
Top-down rooms built from floor types (plank, tile, carpet, lino) with simple coloured furniture rectangles, small signs, soft drop shadows, warm lamp tint at night. Rooms: apartment (lobby + Apt 12), grocery, diner, clinic, pharmacy, bank, bookshop, bakery, hardware, cinema, salon, laundromat, bike shop, cafe, pizza place, dentist, five workplaces (cs, eng, nurse, biz, cook), and the Lakeview Elementary school (hall, classrooms, library, lounge, office, nurse, cafeteria, gym).

## Ambition list
- **Materials that look like materials**: real wood-plank floors with grain, knots, plank-length variation, gaps and a sheen; herringbone/parquet where fitting; tile with grout, per-tile tone variation and scuffs; carpet with weave texture and wear paths; lino with speckle and wax highlights; concrete with trowel marks and cracks; rugs with woven borders, fringe and pattern; wall paint with slight vertical gradient, wainscoting, baseboards with depth, crown mould, switches/outlets, scuff marks, picture rails; windows with depth, sills, curtains and a live sky view.
- **Lighting indoors**: window light pools falling across the floor with the sun angle (use the existing time/sky state), pendant/ceiling lamps with soft pools and a warm bloom, lamp-shade glow, TV/monitor/screen glow on nearby surfaces, neon (cinema, diner) tinting nearby floors, ambient occlusion in corners and under every piece of furniture, contact shadows with correct offset, subtle vignette in corners. Day/night difference must be dramatic and lovely. All through the room's existing lighting hook (read `22-interior-render.js` "indoor lighting").
- **Furniture with form**: each type gets a 3/4-style top-down read — visible front faces and thickness (a few px of side face), top highlight, material texture (leather, fabric weave with tufting, wood grain, brushed steel, chrome, glass), cushions with seams and creases, bed with rumpled quilt, pillows, headboard, nightstand lamp; fridge with handles and magnets; stove with burners and pots; sink with faucet, water highlight; desks with keyboards, mugs, notebooks, plants, lamps, monitors with glowing screens; shelves with convincingly varied spines/jars/cans/boxes; counters with registers; machines (popcorn, jukebox, claw machine, washers/dryers with tumbling clothes, dental chair, hospital beds, server racks with blinking LEDs, CNC/lathe, ranges, vault door); plants with leaf shapes; clutter props (coffee cups, papers, bags, books) as decoration.
- **Wall art and signage**: crisp lettering with depth, chalkboards with believable chalk, posters, frames, clocks with moving hands (if drawn live), mirror reflections with gloss, string lights with glow.
- **Doors/exits/rugs/mats**: exit door with frame, light spill, EXIT sign glow; welcome mats.
- **Per-room identity**: each shop should feel like itself within one glance (bakery warm and floury, pharmacy clean and clinical-green, cinema plush velvet and gold, laundromat bright and steamy-blue, bike shop industrial with hanging wheels, cafe cosy with string lights, pizza place red-green gingham with oven glow, dentist calm teal, bank marble and brass). The five workplaces keep their themes (cs server racks + beanbags, eng machine shop, nurse hospital ward, biz boardroom, cook commercial kitchen).
- **School** (`17-school-draw.js`): the hallway with lockers (door details, vents, stickers), bulletin boards, banners, trophy cabinet, classroom desks/whiteboards, library stacks, gym floor with court lines/sheen, cafeteria details, kids' art.

## Technical notes
- A room's static layer is cached (see `22-interior-render.js` "one cached layer for the static part"): paint heavy detail there once; keep live things (screens, blinking lights, steam, tumbling clothes, people) in the per-frame layer and cheap.
- `defInt` rooms have `floors`, `walls`, `deco`, `furn`, `spots`, `folk` — study `20-interior-core.js` (not yours) and `6282` onward to see how data feeds the painters. Furniture painters are keyed by `t:` type names; keep every existing type name working and make each look much richer. If you introduce new decorative painter types, register them only for your own decoration use.
- Screens, monitors and signs show text: keep text legible and spelled exactly as before.
- Interiors are viewed zoomed in (see baseline: a large apartment lobby fills the screen) — details at 1–2 px must hold up at that zoom, and the whole room must still look composed when zoomed out on small screens.
