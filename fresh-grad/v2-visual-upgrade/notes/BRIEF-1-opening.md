## Ambition: make the cutscene feel like the opening of a beautiful indie game
**Procedural scene (must stand alone; this is what ships if no art exists)**
- **A real camera.** Treat the 1280×720 stage as a world and move a virtual camera: slow establishing push-in with parallax, a track along with the Graduate during the walk, a push to a two-shot for the handshake, a **close-up insert** on the clasped hands and the diploma (drawn large with real detail: paper fibres, ribbon, wax seal), a pull-back/crane up for the hat toss with a hint of slow motion at the toss, a final slow drift. Parallax layers: sky, far hills/trees and a distant clock tower / campus, stage back wall, stage, characters, foreground heads, foreground bokeh/confetti. Layer-depth blur (cheap: pre-blurred sprite copies) for background and foreground.
- **Cinematic frame**: animated letterbox bars (in at the start, out for the cards), a colour grade that moves from cool/anticipatory to warm/triumphant, film grain, subtle chromatic edge softening, vignette, soft bloom on lights and the sun, dust motes in the spot beams, lens flare when the sun enters frame, camera-flash pops in the crowd with tiny rim-light flickers.
- **Characters** (`drawGrad` and the crowd) at far higher fidelity: proper gown with pleats and sleeves that swing, stole with gold edge highlight, mortarboard with thickness and tassel physics (pendulum), faces with eyes/brows/mouth/blush that emote (nervous → proud → ecstatic), hair with volume, hands with fingers where it matters (the handshake, the diploma), shoes, walk cycle with weight shift and cloth lag, secondary motion (tassel, stole, gown hem). The Dean with warmth (laugh lines, silver hair, glasses optional but keep the silver hair/black gown/gold stole). Eight classmates individualised (heights, hair, skin tones from the existing arrays, one with glasses, one blowing a kiss, one crying happy), reacting to the walk (turning heads) and cheering. Colours from `IMAGE_PROMPTS.md` §1–2 (gown `#1d2e6b`, stole `#f0b429`, cap `#0f1626`, Dean `#1b1b21`/`#d4a017`, skin `#e8b48f` for the Graduate, hair `#2a1f18`). The Graduate keeps being the same person who later wears the coral tee.
- **Set**: wood stage with grain/seams/scuffs and a reflection sheen under the spots; curtains with volumetric folds, tie-backs, gold tassel fringe and light/shadow gradients; valance with gold braid; the **banner "FRESH GRAD STATE / CLASS OF 2026"** (keep this text, drawn with `signText`/a real serif+display type; embossed gold edge, light sweep); bunting that sways in wind with fabric shading; podium with carved detail, medallion, microphone with a tiny glint; flowers/ribbons; sky with layered volumetric clouds drifting, sun glare, birds, hills with tree texture, a brick clock tower, lamp-posts and balloons; folding chairs and an audience of three tiers with variety (families, a toddler on shoulders, someone filming on a phone with a glowing screen, handmade blank sign boards), the crowd animates in waves and goes wild with raised arms at the toss.
- **Hat toss + confetti**: caps tumble with proper 3D-ish rotation (flip the diamond's aspect as they rotate), motion trails at the apex, a slow-mo window around 11.6–12.4, tassels trailing, confetti pieces with flutter (cos-modulated width), depth layers (near pieces bigger/blurrier, sparkles with additive glow), streamer curls, caps falling back into the foreground.
- **Typography**: captions in a refined glass plate (speaker label in gold mono caps, text in DM Sans) with a clean typewriter and a soft shadow; the two black cards ("Diploma in hand." / "Now, what does it say?") with presence (large display type, gold hairline, fine grain, a slow vignette breathing, a rolled diploma silhouette drawn between them).
- **Sound** (low volume, respects `G.sound`; build helpers on `SFX.ctx` in your file): a soft ambient crowd murmur and birds, a rising string-like swell during the walk (a few detuned triangle/sine oscillators), a warm chord on the handshake, camera shutter clicks, a whoosh for the toss, a final resonant chord on the white flash. Existing `SFX.applause` calls stay.
- **Determinism**: `drawCutScene(ct)` must be a pure function of `ct` (seeded RNG, no state accumulated frame-to-frame) so jumping `CUT.t` (the test harness does this) renders correctly, and `Skip` at any time is safe. Cache heavy sprites (blurred layers, crowd, curtains) in offscreen canvases built once in `startCutscene`.
- Respect `REDUCED` (no flashes/strobing, calmer camera) and phone sizes (cover-fit the 16:9 stage to any viewport; at portrait aspect, crop/pan to the action rather than shrinking into a tiny letterbox; captions never overlap faces).
