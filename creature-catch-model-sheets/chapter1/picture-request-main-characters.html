Creature Catch · Chapter 1 · people

Picture request: the two main characters

The hero you play, as the boy and as the girl. They're the first people made with the paint-and-bake method, so they're also the test of how well people work. Each one needs five pictures: a character sheet, then four HD views made from it.

  1. Use the same ChatGPT chat as the creatures, so the paint style matches.
  2. For each character: save both reference pictures, attach them to prompt 1, then send prompts 2 to 5 in order.
  3. Check each view against the list under it, and re-roll any view that breaks a rule.
  4. Bring back all 10 pictures, named as listed (a zip per character is fine).

Why the A-pose: arms a little out from the body and feet apart, the same in every view, so every arm and leg can be traced on its own and the skeleton can move it.

The player: boy

1.55 m tall

1 · Character sheet (attach the pictures)
We're making the people for a cosy creature-collecting game. The creatures are already done as painted 3D-style art; now the people need to match them. Make a character design sheet for the boy hero, the hero you play (boy look). The attached images are the original pixel-art sprite with a close-up of the face, and a pixel-art turnaround sheet showing the character from every angle. Where they differ, follow the sprite. Keep the same design, outfit, colours and personality, but redraw them as clean, high-definition painted concept art, not pixel art.

THE PAGE
Landscape, 1536 x 1024. On the left, a large front three-quarter hero view. On the right, small front, side and back views in a row, all the same size with the feet on the same ground line, with the side view facing left. Below them, close-ups of the face, a hand and a shoe. Next to the small views, a height bar in metres showing 1.55 m. The name "HERO (BOY)" at the top. No other text.

THE CHARACTER
A young adventurer, 1.55 m tall: a teenager with friendly hero proportions.
- Hair: messy, spiky brown hair in big chunky locks.
- Face: warm brown eyes and a confident, friendly smile.
- Clothes: a green explorer jacket worn open over a cream shirt, sleeves pushed up a little; a brown belt with a purple-and-blue capture cube clipped on the LEFT hip; khaki cargo trousers; brown lace-up boots.
- Backpack: a small brown leather backpack with a flap and two shoulder straps.
- Hands: bare, fingers together.

POSE
Standing straight in a relaxed A-pose, the same in every view: arms straight down and held about 30 degrees out from the body, so there is a clear gap of background between each arm and the body; hands relaxed with the fingers together; feet about shoulder-width apart with a clear gap between the legs; looking straight ahead with a friendly expression. Hands empty.

THE LOOK (the same for every person in this game): a cosy creature-collecting adventure game. Friendly stylised proportions, about five heads tall (a slightly large head, big warm eyes), simple shapes that read clearly, like the hero of a modern cosy JRPG. Stylised hand-painted surfaces with clear blocks of colour and gentle cloth and leather texture, not photoreal, matching the game's painted creatures. Colours true to the original sprite. No black outlines. Soft, even, shadowless studio light with only small highlights. Plain flat light-grey background, exactly #C8C8CA, with no floor, shadow, reflection, border or watermark.
2 · HD front view
Now make the HD views of the boy from this sheet, one picture per view. Start with the FRONT view.

Rules for every view:
- True orthographic, like a technical drawing: no perspective and no tilt. For this one, the camera looks dead at their front from chest height.
- Portrait, 1024 x 1536. The whole person in frame with about a 5% margin, centred left to right, nothing cut off. In every view, at the same scale: the soles of the feet on the same pixel row, and the top of the head (or hat) on the same pixel row.
- Exactly the same person as the sheet: same shapes, same clothes, same colours on the same parts in every view.
- The same relaxed A-pose in every view, with clear gaps of background between the arms and the body and between the legs. Hands empty, fingers together.
- Nothing hair-thin: hair in chunky locks at least 3 cm across; straps, laces and belts at least 2 cm wide. Nothing see-through, blurred or flashing. No sparkles or particles in the air.
- No text.

THE LOOK (the same for every person in this game): a cosy creature-collecting adventure game. Friendly stylised proportions, about five heads tall (a slightly large head, big warm eyes), simple shapes that read clearly, like the hero of a modern cosy JRPG. Stylised hand-painted surfaces with clear blocks of colour and gentle cloth and leather texture, not photoreal, matching the game's painted creatures. Colours true to the original sprite. No black outlines. Soft, even, shadowless studio light with only small highlights. Plain flat light-grey background, exactly #C8C8CA, with no floor, shadow, reflection, border or watermark.
3 · HD side view (facing left)
Same again for the SIDE view of the boy: the camera dead at their side, the person facing LEFT (their nose toward the left edge of the picture), so we see their left side. Same scale, same ground line and same top line as the front view, same paint and detail. All the same rules.
4 · HD back view
Same again for the BACK view of the boy: the camera dead behind them, looking at their back. Same scale, same ground line and same top line as the front view, same paint and detail. All the same rules.
5 · HD top view
Same again for the TOP view of the boy: the camera straight down from above, looking at the top of their head and shoulders, with their face and toes pointing toward the TOP edge of the picture. Square, 1024 x 1024, with the whole person in frame. Same paint and detail. All the same rules.
