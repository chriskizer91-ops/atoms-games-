Creature Catch · paint-and-bake · round 2

Picture request: Mushling, Drizzlon, Lunarion

Three creatures from simplest to hardest, for the small-model-only test. Each one needs five pictures: a character sheet, then four HD views made from it. The rules are the same as for Coalossus.

  1. Use one ChatGPT chat (the Coalossus chat is ideal, so the style matches). Finish one creature before starting the next.
  2. For each creature: save its reference sprite, attach it to prompt 1, then send prompts 2 to 5 in order.
  3. Check each view against the list under it and re-roll any view that breaks a rule.
  4. Bring back all 15 pictures, named as listed (a zip is fine).

About the wings and tail. Drizzlon's and Lunarion's wings are asked for flat, held out to the sides and facing forward, like a banner. The front picture then shows the whole wing and the side picture shows a thin edge, which the method turns into a clean, thin wing. Swept-back wings would come out as thick wedges. Drizzlon's tail curls straight up behind it for the same reason.

A mushroom creature that peeks out from under its spotted cap and darts back if you look at it.

0.6 m tall with a cap 0.55 m wide

THE CREATURE
Mushling is a small, shy mushroom creature, 0.6 m tall, with a cap 0.55 m wide.
- Cap: a big domed toadstool cap, glossy bright red with large round cream-white spots, the rim curving down a little all around. It is wider than the body and overhangs it like an umbrella. Under the rim, chunky pale tan gills (drawn as thick ribs, not thin lines).
- Body: a soft, round, cream-coloured body like a short, stubby mushroom stalk, a little wider at the bottom, peeking out from under the cap.
- Face: on the upper body just under the cap rim: two big round dark-brown eyes with white highlights, and rosy pink cheeks.
- Arms: two short, chubby arms, with both little hands raised in front of its mouth, shyly.
- Feet: two small round feet, a little apart, with a clear gap between them.
- Build: very chubby and simple; the cap is about half of its total height.

POSE
Standing upright facing straight ahead, both hands held up in front of its mouth, feet a little apart with a clear gap of background between them. The same pose in every view.

THE LOOK (the same for every creature in this game): a friendly creature-collecting game. Chunky, rounded, cute-but-mighty proportions, big warm expressive eyes and simple shapes that read clearly. Stylised hand-painted surfaces with clear blocks of colour and gentle texture, not photoreal. Colours true to the original sprite. No black outlines around the creature or any of its parts. Soft, even, shadowless studio light with only small highlights. Glowing parts glow, with no halo onto the background. Plain flat light-grey background, exactly #C8C8CA, with no floor, shadow, reflection, border or watermark.

- The same pose in every view, with clear gaps of background between separate parts (legs, arms, wings).
- Nothing hair-thin: every part, spike, feather and tuft at least 5 cm across. Nothing see-through, smoky, blurred or flashing. No sparkles or particles in the air.
