Creature Catch · paint-and-bake · chapter 1

Picture request: Mossback, Embroak, Clovermouse, Puffmunk, Fleecling

Five creatures from the first chapter: the other two starters (Drizzlon is done) and the first three creatures on Meadow Path. Each one needs five pictures: a character sheet, then four HD views made from it. The rules are the same as before.

  1. Use one ChatGPT chat (the Coalossus chat is ideal, so the style matches). Finish one creature before starting the next.
  2. For each creature: save its reference sprite, attach it to prompt 1, then send prompts 2 to 5 in order.
  3. Check each view against the list under it and re-roll any view that breaks a rule.
  4. Bring back all 25 pictures, named as listed (a zip per creature is fine).

About the poses. Clovermouse and Puffmunk sit up on their hind legs, as in their sprites. Mossback, Embroak and Fleecling stand or sit on all fours. Tails curl straight up behind, and thin parts (tongue, tail) are asked for thick, so the method can model them cleanly.

- Tail: a short, stubby pointed tail.
- Build: low and round, with the shell about two thirds of its height.
